About Us

Living Potential supports wellbeing, learning and connection through nature-based activities on and around the farm.

Our mission

Our mission is to create supportive outdoor spaces where people can learn, grow in confidence and improve wellbeing through meaningful contact with nature.

We aim to make these opportunities welcoming, inclusive and easier to access for the local community.

Who we are

Living Potential is a non-profit social enterprise based on a working farm near Wetherby and Leeds. We work with individuals, families and groups through practical outdoor activities, care farming and community projects.

Our work brings together wellbeing support, education and community engagement in a calm natural setting.
